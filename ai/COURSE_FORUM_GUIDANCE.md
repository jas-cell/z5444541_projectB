## 3. Design implications

1. **One product, two hand-ins.** Part B is not a new generic portfolio exercise. Signal Harbour must read as the same product introduced in Part A.
2. **Reuse Kaiyuan’s Project A foundation.** Cleaning rules, calendars, return definition, headline mapping, deduplication, Attention Pulse equation, sample window, and product identity must carry forward.
3. **No waiting for Part A marks.** Concurrent marking means Part B must self-audit Part A before extending it.
4. **Custom augmented finVADER is encouraged.** Week 9 / open-source finVADER may be used as a benchmark, but the course convenor expressly pushed students toward designing their own AI-assisted augmentation.
5. **“Custom” requires evidence.** A tiny undocumented dictionary update is not enough for the innovation band. The model must be implemented, validated, ablated, and critically evaluated.
6. **Best innovation path is continuous.** Connect Part A Attention Pulse → Part B sentiment model → lagged portfolio fusion → Streamlit explainability into one Signal Harbour contribution.
